Walk: Church & Bible Study Log

Support for the iPhone app. Walk is a record of the church services and Bible studies you attend, with sermon notes. There is no account.

Common questions

I forgot to log Sunday. Tap “Log church or Bible study” and change the day. You can log up to two weeks back.

I logged something by mistake. Press and hold the entry on the main screen, then choose Remove.

Where are my sermon notes? The latest one is on the main screen under “Last sermon”. Tap “All sermon notes” to see every note.

Does Walk+ unlock logging? No. Logging, sermon notes, and your record are free. Walk+ adds a seasonal challenge and practice templates.

Privacy

Walk stores your attendance record, sermon notes, practices, and giving on this iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
