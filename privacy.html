Privacy Policy

Walk: A Rule of Life is a local iPhone app. It does not create an account and does not run a Walk backend.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

What stays on your phone

Your name, reminder time, which practices you turned on, and your practice logs (including optional notes and giving) are stored in Apple’s on-device database. Giving is never compared and never sent by Walk to a server.

What leaves the phone

If you buy Walk+, Apple’s StoreKit processes the purchase. Walk does not receive your payment card details. Optional Walk+ features use Apple’s subscription status on this device.

If you allow notifications, iOS schedules a local reminder. Walk does not send push through a Walk server.
